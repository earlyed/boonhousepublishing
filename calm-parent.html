The Five Core Tools

Skills that replace the yell — not willpower

Tool 01
The Two-Breath Reset
Inhale for four counts. Exhale for six. A slow, complete exhale activates the parasympathetic nervous system — the rest-and-regulate branch — before you speak. This is not a metaphor. It is neurological.
"You cannot think your way out of yelling. You can breathe your way back to a state where thinking becomes possible."
Tool 02
The Prepared Environment
The environment teaches before the adult speaks. Reduce what's visible, create predictable stations, build a calm corner. Environmental changes feel passive — that's precisely why they work when nothing else does.
"The home that runs smoothly is not the one with the most rules. It is the one with the most predictability."
Tool 03
Build Discipline, Don't Demand It
There's a crucial difference between a child who does something because they're told and a child who knows how to do it. Skills are built through model, practise together, support with less intervention, release.
"I'll put away five things, then you put away five things. Ready? I'll start."
Tool 04
The Ten-Minute Repair
You are going to yell again. The question is what happens next. Research on rupture and repair shows children who experience consistent repair after rupture develop stronger emotional security than children who experience no rupture at all.
"I got too loud. That wasn't fair. Let's try that again."
Tool 05
Boundary Plus Choice
The negotiation spiral happens because resistance has learned to produce concessions. Boundary plus choice ends it: the limit is non-negotiable, the choice within it is real.
"Time to get dressed. Shirt or trousers first? If you don't choose in ten seconds, I'll choose."

Common Questions

What parents ask about calm parenting

Practical answers, not philosophy

How do I stop yelling at my kids? ▾
Yelling is a symptom of four root causes: a nervous system running on empty, inherited scripts from your own childhood, a skills gap nobody talks about, and a developmental mismatch between what you expect and what your child's brain can do. The Calm Parent addresses all four through a practical 8-week system — starting with the Two-Breath Reset, a neurologically grounded technique that interrupts the stress response before the yell happens. You cannot think your way out of yelling. The book gives you the tools to interrupt it physically, before thought is required.
What is gentle parenting and does it work? ▾
Gentle parenting prioritises connection, understanding, and age-appropriate expectations over punishment and control. Research consistently finds it produces better long-term outcomes for children's emotional regulation, self-esteem, and behaviour. The Calm Parent is a practical gentle parenting system — not a philosophy to agree with, but a specific set of tools to implement, with exact language for common triggers and a week-by-week implementation guide.
How do I manage screen time without constant battles? ▾
The issue isn't the device — it's what the device has replaced. Chapter Five reframes screen battles entirely: rather than fighting against screens, you build competing conditions that make screens less central. The Boundary Plus Choice script ends the negotiation spiral: "Timer went off. You can do Legos or go outside." The follow-through — doing it without negotiation every single time — is where the system is built or broken.
What is the Ten-Minute Repair? ▾
A three-part tool for after yelling or rupture. First: name what you did wrong, without excuses. Second: name the impact briefly — one sentence. Third: redo — repeat the original interaction the way you wanted to do it. This third part is what most parents miss. Research on rupture and repair shows children who experience consistent repair after rupture develop stronger emotional security than children who experience no rupture at all.
How is this different from other gentle parenting books? ▾
Most parenting books are excellent at rules. Stay calm. Be consistent. Set boundaries with warmth. The Calm Parent starts where those books end: with the exact tools, the exact scripts, and the exact weekly sequence for building those rules into habits. It includes a complete 8-week Focus Guide that takes the tools from all fifteen chapters and sequences them in the order that makes implementation possible — not overwhelming.
