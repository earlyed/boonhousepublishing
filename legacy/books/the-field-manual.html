The Field Manual

A Working System for Money, Built for the Man Who Keeps It in the Truck

By BoonHouse Publishing

This title is currently in active development. It is being built as an operations manual for personal finance rather than a motivational read: second person, direct, treating the reader as a competent operator whose system has a specific fault, not as a patient who needs encouragement. Each chapter is planned around a cold open naming the cost of doing nothing in the reader's own numbers, followed by the mechanism behind the failure and a concrete fix.

The working premise is that most financial advice for men fails because it borrows the tone of either a lifestyle guide or a lecture, and this book is built instead like a manual for a piece of equipment that has a fault everyone can name once it is pointed out: lifestyle creep, load bearing debt, the leak that never gets fixed because it never gets diagnosed. Dry humor is allowed in single lines. Pep talk is not.

Full chapter content, named frameworks, and a description written from finished manuscript material will replace this notice once the book is further along. Check back, or see the BoonHouse Publishing Amazon storefront for current releases in the meantime.
