About

Gentle books, built on what actually works

BoonHouse Publishing makes calm, Montessori-aligned picture books for toddlers, and evidence-based parenting books for the adults raising them.

What we make

Our children's books use real photographs of real children, not illustrations, paired with calm, respectful language. Each one is built around a specific moment a toddler actually goes through: a first haircut, a new sibling, a meltdown over losing a game, learning to say sorry. The goal is always the same: give a child language for something they're already feeling, before or during the moment it happens.

Our parenting books are written for the adults on the other side of those same moments, with practical scripts rather than abstract philosophy - what to actually say when a toddler is mid-tantrum, mid-meltdown, or refusing to get in the car seat.

How we work

We're a small, independent publisher. Our children's books draw on Montessori principles around respecting a child's real experience rather than minimizing it. Our blog and book content go through a structured editorial process before anything is published - every article and script is checked against a written style and accuracy standard before it goes live.
