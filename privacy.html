Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026.

What this site does not do

BoonHouse Publishing does not operate user accounts, does not process payments, and does not collect names, email addresses, or any personal information through this website. There are no forms on this site that request personal data. Purchases happen entirely on Amazon, under Amazon's own privacy policy, not ours.

Analytics

This site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count anonymous pageviews. GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not track individuals across sites. It records only aggregate information such as which pages were viewed, approximate location (country/region level), and referring site.

Pinterest

This site includes Pinterest's official save button widget, which allows visitors to save images to their own Pinterest account. Pinterest may set cookies and collect data when this widget is used, governed by Pinterest's own privacy policy, not this site's.

External links

This site links to Amazon and other third-party sites for purchases and further reading. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact details on our Amazon author page.
