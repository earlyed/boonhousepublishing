Why We Still Create Simple Children's Books in a World Full of AI

How BoonHouse Publishing creates Montessori-aligned children's books with real photographs and human editorial judgement, without AI in book production.

By BoonHouse Publishing · 2026-10-27

Behind BoonHouse Publishing's Montessori aligned children's books, our careful selection of photographs, and the human experience behind every page.

Creating children's books is meaningful work, but it also requires patience, judgement, and a willingness to keep learning. Not every decision will appeal to every reader. Not every book will meet every person's expectations. And sometimes, a comment from someone who has never seen the care behind a book can stay with its creators longer than it should.

We understand that publishing means opening our work to public opinion. Readers are entitled to their views, and thoughtful criticism can help us improve. But behind every book is a team of people who have considered its purpose, its intended reader, and the small details that can make a difference to a young child.

At BoonHouse Publishing, we continue to create because we believe that simple books can serve an important purpose in childhood.

Every BoonHouse Publishing book is created by people, without the use of AI at any stage of book creation or production. Our writing, content, themes, editorial decisions, and photographs are all developed or selected by people. We do not use AI generated text, images, or other AI generated content in our books.

Why We Believe Simple Children's Books Matter

Young children do not need every page to be crowded with information. Sometimes, one clear photograph and one carefully chosen sentence can give a child the opportunity to notice, understand, and connect.

A book about feelings, for example, does not need to explain every aspect of emotional development at once. A book introducing everyday experiences does not need to turn each page into a lesson. For young readers, simplicity can make an idea easier to recognise and discuss with a trusted adult.

This is one reason our work is informed by Montessori principles. We value clarity, purposeful presentation, real world experiences, and respect for the child's developing understanding.

Montessori aligned does not mean that every child will respond to a book in the same way. Children develop at different rates, and families have different needs. Our aim is to create thoughtful resources that parents, caregivers, and educators can use in ways that suit the child in front of them.

The Care Behind Our Photographs

Choosing photographs for a children's book is not simply a matter of finding an attractive image.

A photograph needs to be relevant to the idea being introduced. It should be clear enough for a young child to understand, appropriate for the intended reader and purpose of the page, and consistent with the purpose of the page. The relationship between the words and the image matters, too.

A beautiful photograph is not necessarily the right photograph. An image can be visually appealing yet confusing, distracting, or poorly suited to the concept a child is learning about.

That is why we take care when selecting and reviewing the photographs in our books. We consider what a young reader is likely to notice, whether the image communicates the intended idea, and whether it belongs naturally within the wider book.

The photographs in our books are not AI generated. They are selected by people as part of our editorial process, with attention to relevance, clarity, and suitability for young readers.

These decisions may seem small, but children's books are made up of small decisions. Each page contributes to the experience of reading the book as a whole.

Why Human Judgement Still Matters

There is more to creating a children's book than assembling words and pictures.

Someone must decide what belongs in the book, what should be left out, whether the language is suitable for the intended reader, and whether the finished pages work together. These decisions require more than producing something that looks complete. They require an understanding of the book's purpose and the child it is intended to serve.

Our approach is informed by human editorial judgement and experience connected with early education. We consider not only what a page communicates, but also how it might be used by a parent, caregiver, or educator in an everyday setting.

We do not believe that a book needs to be complicated to be worthwhile. Nor do we believe that every educational resource needs to make grand promises. We would rather create a book with a clear purpose than make claims about what it can achieve that we cannot substantiate.

What Negative Comments Can Teach Us

Publishing work publicly means accepting that not everyone will like what we create. Some criticism points to a genuine weakness and deserves careful consideration. Other comments reflect different preferences, expectations, or ideas about what a children's book should be.

We try to distinguish between the two.

We cannot make every reader agree with our choices, and we do not expect every book to suit every family. What we can do is continue to review our work, listen where feedback is constructive, and remain responsible for the decisions we make.

It can be difficult when a dismissive comment overshadows the hours spent developing a book. Yet a single opinion does not tell the whole story of why a book was created or whether it may be useful to the families who choose it.

Our responsibility is not to win every argument. It is to keep creating with care, to be honest about our process, and to make improvements when they are warranted.

Are BoonHouse Publishing Books AI Generated?

No. We do not use AI at any stage of creating or producing our books. Our writing, content, themes, editorial decisions, and photographs are all developed or selected by people. We do not use AI generated text, images, or other AI generated content in our books.

We believe readers should be able to understand how the books they purchase are made. Our position is straightforward: our books are created through human work, experience, and judgement, from the development of the content to the selection of the photographs.

We want families to have clear information about the work behind our books, without ambiguity about our production process.

Why We Will Continue

We create books for young children because their everyday experiences matter. Learning to recognise an emotion, understand a familiar object, approach a new situation, or talk with a trusted adult can be part of a child's growing understanding of the world.

We cannot promise that every book will be right for every child. We can commit to approaching our work thoughtfully, explaining our intentions honestly, and continuing to learn from the people who read our books.

There will always be different opinions about what makes a good children's book. We accept that. We will keep listening to constructive feedback, refining our work, and creating books with a clear purpose.

Because behind every simple page is a decision about what a child might need, what a parent might find helpful, and what deserves to be said with care.

And that work is worth continuing.

FAQ

Does BoonHouse Publishing use AI to create its books?

No. We do not use AI at any stage of creating or producing our books. Our writing, content, themes, and editorial decisions are the work of people.

Are the photographs in BoonHouse Publishing books AI generated?

No. The photographs in every book are selected by people, not generated by AI. They are reviewed for relevance, clarity, and suitability for young readers.

What makes BoonHouse Publishing books Montessori aligned?

Our approach values clear presentation, purposeful learning, real world experiences, and respect for children's developing understanding. We aim to create books that parents, caregivers, and educators can use to support everyday learning and conversation.

Why are some BoonHouse Publishing books so simple?

Young children can benefit from clear language and focused images that help them recognise and explore one idea at a time. We aim to avoid unnecessary complexity when it would distract from the purpose of a page.

Who are BoonHouse Publishing books created for?

Our children's books are intended to support young readers and the adults who share books with them. Each title's description explains its purpose and themes so families can decide whether it suits their child.

Does BoonHouse Publishing welcome feedback?

Yes. Constructive feedback helps us consider how our books can be improved. We recognise that families have different preferences and that no single book will suit every child. We aim to consider criticism thoughtfully while remaining clear about our publishing principles.
