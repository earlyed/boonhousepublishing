The Lie, the Wiring, the Protocol

Dismantling the Sentences Men Say Instead of the Truth

This title is currently in active development. Its structure is built around myth demolition: every chapter opens with one sentence men actually say, alone on its own line, in quotes, such as I'm fine, or I just need to push through, then dismantles what saying that sentence actually costs, stated concretely rather than in the abstract.

The planned register is a straight talking clinician and coach voice: plain declarative sentences, short paragraphs, full respect for the reader, and zero therapy language borrowed from other books on this shelf. The book talks to the man reading it, not about him in the third person, and is built to never soften a fact or moralize about one. Each chapter's middle section, The Wiring, explains the nervous system or conditioning mechanism behind why the opening lie feels true from inside, naming researchers, institutions, and years rather than gesturing at general psychology.

Full chapter content, named frameworks, and a description written from finished manuscript material will replace this notice once the book is further along. Check back, or see the BoonHouse Publishing Amazon storefront for current releases in the meantime.
